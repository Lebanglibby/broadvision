## Services

1. **Comprehensive Eye Examinations**
   Thorough visual acuity tests and ocular health assessments for adults and children to ensure optimal clarity and early detection of changes.

2. **Pediatric Optometry**
   Friendly, gentle eye care tailored specifically for young patients, supporting healthy visual development for school and play.

3. **Prescription Eyewear & Lens Fitting**
   Custom fitting for single-vision, progressive, reading, and protective blue-light filtering lenses framed in styles to suit every face shape.

4. **Contact Lens Assessments & Fitting**
   Professional evaluation and fitting for soft lenses, daily disposables, astigmatism correction, and multifocal contact lenses.

5. **Dry Eye & Ocular Comfort Therapy**
   Targeted relief and care plans for persistent dry, tired, or irritated eyes caused by screen use, environmental factors, or allergies.

6. **Screenings & Preventative Care**
   Non-invasive screenings to monitor eye health, intraocular pressure, and retinal wellness over time.

7. **Specialist Referrals**
   Where conditions require advanced ophthalmology intervention, we provide appropriate referrals to ensure continuity of care for our patients.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How often should I get a comprehensive eye exam?**
We recommend a comprehensive eye exam once every 12 to 24 months for adults. Children and seniors should have annual exams or follow their optometrist's recommended schedule.

**Do I need an appointment, or do you accept walk-ins?**
While we accommodate walk-ins for frame browsing and minor adjustments whenever possible, we recommend booking an appointment in advance to secure your preferred exam time without waiting.

**What should I bring to my appointment?**
Please bring your current prescription glasses, sunglasses, contact lens information (if applicable), and a list of any current medications.

**How long does a typical eye exam take?**
A standard comprehensive vision examination usually takes between 30 and 45 minutes, allowing ample time for tests and clear discussion with your optometrist.

**Do you offer blue-light filtering lenses?**
Yes, we offer specialized anti-reflective blue-light coatings for both prescription lenses and non-prescription computer glasses to help reduce digital eye fatigue.

**Do you refer patients to ophthalmologists when needed?**
Yes. Where conditions require advanced specialist intervention, we provide appropriate referrals to ensure our patients receive the best possible continuity of care.
